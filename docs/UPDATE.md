# Future Work

- Move HUB75 brightness out of the colours. Scaling colours ahead of the four-bit panel leaves each channel about four levels at the default brightness of 3, so shadow stops drop out. The pinned driver's output-enable window can't take over alone: it centres the window, so it opens only even clock counts, ten sizes up to the tuned 20, and colour scaling on top of it makes dim colours step backwards between levels. Fifteen distinct levels at full colour depth need single-clock windows, which means patching the driver, whose framebuffer is private; the alternative is ten perceptually spaced levels. Either needs a panel to judge.
- Run this firmware on Matrix.64. Check that fades start without a visible step. Measure the collection's frame rates at full resolution: a pattern whose work grows with its pixels takes sixteen times as long at 64×64 as on Matrix.16, where most draw a frame in under 1.5 ms, Twisted Tunnel and Collatz Kelp in about 2 ms and Accretion in 4.3 ms. The pattern code still divides floats in software from about 110 places and calls libm from about 100, not all of them per pixel; moving those out of pixel loops is the first lever if 64×64 falls short. Try the USB bridge on the MatrixPortal S3.
- Run this firmware on Matrix.22. Its patterns and thumbnails were judged only from host renders, and `show()` leaves about 2 ms per frame to draw at 60 fps there, so patterns that draw for more than about 1 ms on Matrix.16 will run slower.
- Send WS2812 frames without blocking. NeoPixel's `show()` waits 8.3 ms per frame on Matrix.16 and about 15 ms on Matrix.22; an RMT transfer in the background would leave most of the frame to drawing and the network.
- Patterns that still read poorly at 16 pixels: Topo Vortex is a dense maze where its two vortices overlap, Tidal Topography's water is dark and low in contrast on the body stop, and Phase Slippage stays textured at its default Aliasing of 82.
- Soliton Lattice Echo, Ripple Cell Grid, Poolside, Pong Wars and Plasma draw by palette position alone, so `tools/test_collection.cpp` cannot tell a control that changes them from one that only shifts their colour; judge their controls by eye.
